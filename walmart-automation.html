Frequently Asked Questions

Walmart requires an active US business structure (LLC/C-Corp), EIN, Sales Tax Reseller Permit, and an existing web footprint. We help configure these for you to maximize approval probability.
WFS is Walmart Fulfillment Services. WFS stores, packs, and delivers products to customers, just like Amazon FBA. It unlocks 2-Day WFS delivery badges, drastically boosting traffic.
Walmart has strict dropshipping rules and will suspend accounts using retail arbitrage. We do NOT use retail arbitrage; we utilize real wholesale supplier invoices, keeping your account safe.
